Every project starts somewhere. Sometimes it’s a notebook full of ideas. Sometimes it’s a conversation that stays with you. For us, it started with a simple question: what would we like to make more room for?

## Start with what matters

We wanted a place to share our work and the thinking behind it. Somewhere that feels welcoming, reads easily, and leaves the focus on the words.

We began with three things:

- A clear introduction to who we are.
- A little context about how we work.
- A space to write as we go.

That felt like enough to begin.

## Leave room for change

There’s no need to work out every detail before taking the first step. A small, useful beginning gives you something to learn from.

> Make the first version clear enough to use and simple enough to change.

We’ll keep adding notes here as our work takes shape. You can read more about [our story](/about) in the meantime.
